| 1 | Architecture | Define Level 6 typology and position it within (and beyond) the Kautz (2022) framework | Level 5 compiles static symbolic rules into the differentiable architecture. Level 6 makes the symbolic rule vocabulary itself evolvable: failures drive discovery of new predicate-space symbols, which generate candidate rules, which are validated and promoted into the rule_base. Kautz's Type 6 describes neural systems that dynamically use symbolic reasoning internally — the direction is established but the standard is not fully formalised. This PoC implements a practical extension toward that direction by making the symbolic vocabulary evolvable on top of a Level 5 baseline: Type 5 architecture with a self-modifying symbolic substrate.
